# Valorium Discord Bot - RPG Commands

Welcome to Valorium, an exciting RPG Discord bot! Engage in epic adventures, battles, and crafting with a variety of commands. Explore a fantasy world and become the ultimate hero. Here are some of the amazing features and commands you can use:

## Getting Started

1. `+token me`: Register your token in Valorium and embark on your journey as a hero.

## Gameplay

2. `+gw`: Claim your free weapon and gear up for battles.

3. `+equip [weaponID]`: Equip your chosen weapon for upcoming challenges.

4. `+play hit`: Participate in events and engage in thrilling battles.

5. `+farm hit`: Gather crafting items by exploring the world and engaging in battles.

6. `+awaken`: Awaken your abilities using normal awakening gems.

7. `+awaken elite`: Utilize Elite awakening gems for a more powerful awakening.

8. `+shop`: Browse the shop to discover exciting items and gear.

9. `+shop buy [itemIDHere]`: Purchase the desired item from the shop.

## Economy

10. `+bal`: Check your balance, including Gold coins, platinum, and keys.

11. `+aps`: View achievements, track your progress, and strive for greatness.

12. `+info`: Get insights into your user profile and stats.

## Updates and Information

13. `+updates`: Stay informed about the latest changes and improvements.

## Inventory and Crafting

14. `+inv`: Explore your inventory and see the treasures you've acquired.

15. `+inv craft`: Manage your crafting inventory and create valuable items.

16. `+wepInfo [weaponID]`: Learn more about a specific weapon's attributes.

17. `+craft`: Discover the items available for crafting.

18. `+craft [itemID]`: Craft unique items to aid you on your journey.

## Rewards and Enhancements

19. `+promocode [code here]`: Redeem special codes to receive valuable rewards.

## Premium Features

20. `+store`: Browse the platinum store for exclusive items and benefits.

21. `+store buy [itemID]`: Purchase premium items from the platinum store.

## About Valorium

Valorium is a free-to-play RPG Discord bot that offers a rich fantasy experience with countless adventures, battles, and crafting opportunities. Platinum is the premium currency that grants access to additional content and items.

Embark on your epic journey in Valorium and become the hero you were destined to be.

Conquer challenges, forge alliances, and emerge as a legendary champion in Valorium!
