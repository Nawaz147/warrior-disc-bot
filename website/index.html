Bot Commands

  • 1. +token me - To register your token in Valorium discord bot
  • 2. +gw - To get your free weapon
  • 3. +equip [weaponID] - To equip your weapon
  • 4. +play hit - To play event
  • 5. +farm hit - To farm crafting items
  • 6. +awaken - to awaken using normal awakening gem
  • 7. +awaken elite - to awaken using Elite awakening gem
  • 8. +shop - to check whats in the shop!
  • 9. +shop buy [itemID] - To buy the item mentioned from shop
  • 10. +bal - To check balance (Gold coins , platinum , keys)
  • 11. +aps - To check achievments left and their progress
  • 12. +info - To check user info
  • 13. +updates - To check latest changes done in the discord bot
  • 14. +inv - To check your inventory
  • 15. +inv craft- To check your crafting inventory
  • 16. +wepInfo [weaponID] - To check weapon info
  • 17. +craft - To check items available for crafting
  • 18. +craft [itemID] - To craft that item
  • 19. +promocode [code here] - To receive the item
  • 20. +store - To see the platinum store
  • 21. +store buy [itemID] - To buy an item from the platinum store
  • 22. +attack [user] - To attack a user
  • 23. +tos - To check terms of service
  • 24. +tos - To accept terms of service
